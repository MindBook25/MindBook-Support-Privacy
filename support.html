Frequently Asked Questions

Open the MindBook app → go to Settings → Privacy Boundaries. Toggle "Private Account" to ON. When enabled, only confirmed friends can view your posts, reflection history, and badges.
Tap "Forgot Password?" on the mobile sign-in screen and enter your registered email. You will receive an official recovery link. Clicking that link opens our secure Account Recovery Portal where you can enter a new password.

Go to Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account inside the app. Alternatively, submit a request on our Account & Data Deletion Portal. All credentials, posts, photos, and messages are permanently purged within 48 hours.
