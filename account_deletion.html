Delete your MindBook account & data

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy laws, you can permanently delete your MindBook account and all associated personal data at any time.

⚠️

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All your reflections, mood logs, uploaded photos, friends connections, and earned badges will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

1 Option A: Delete from the MindBook Mobile App

If you have access to your mobile phone or tablet:

  1. Open the MindBook app.
  2. Tap your profile avatar or the Settings icon in the top right.
  3. Select Account Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Enter your password to verify ownership. Your account is deleted immediately.

2 Option B: Submit a Web Deletion Request (Without App)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, fill out the official verification form below:

✓ Deletion Request Submitted

We have generated a deletion ticket. A verification confirmation has been dispatched to your email address. All associated database records and media files will be permanently purged within 48 hours.

What data is deleted?

Once your deletion request is processed, the following items are permanently destroyed:

  • Your account credentials, password salt, and email authentication records.
  • Profile information, biographical text, and uploaded avatar/banner images.
  • All authored reflections, diary posts, and comments.
  • All private mood logs, gratitude entries, and challenge streak histories.
  • Friendship graphs and notification tokens.

Questions? Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@mindbook.in.
